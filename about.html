About Us

Who we are and what drives our products.

Our Story

Life Style Ceramics supplies sanitaryware, urinals and washroom automation to commercial and residential clients across South Africa. We work with facilities managers, contractors and homeowners who need reliable, hygienic fittings backed by real technical support — not just a product listing.

From hand dryers and automatic taps to sensor flushers and full washroom fit-outs, we source and supply equipment built for durability in high-traffic environments, with pricing and availability confirmed directly by our team for every order.

What We Do

We supply, quote and help specify washroom automation and sanitaryware — hand dryers, automatic taps, soap dispensers, sensor flushers and urinals, entrance automation, and the accessories that go with a complete commercial washroom. Every quote is reviewed by our team before it's sent, so pricing and stock availability are confirmed, not automated guesswork.

Privacy Policy

We collect only the information you give us directly — your name, contact details, and the items you've enquired or requested a quote about — to process your order, respond to your enquiry, and keep a record of past correspondence with you. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Information is retained only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for (e.g. quote history, order records) and is stored on infrastructure we control directly. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us via the contact form, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Quality Policy

We select products for durability, water-efficiency and reliable performance in high-traffic commercial environments. Stock availability and specifications are confirmed directly with suppliers before any quote is finalized, so what you're quoted reflects what's actually available — not a generic catalog listing.

If a product you've received doesn't match what was quoted or has a manufacturing fault, contact us directly and we'll work with you to resolve it.
